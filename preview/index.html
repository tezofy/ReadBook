The Author

Nocturne

Dark romance for readers who like their love story with teeth.

I write about the kind of love that should not be survivable — the ones where somebody is running and somebody is waiting, and they meet in the worst possible room. My characters are not victims and they are not villains. They are people who did a terrible thing for a good reason and then had to live inside it.

Before I wrote full-time I spent eleven years as a hospital night-shift administrator, which is where I learned that the most frightening thing in the world is not a locked door. It is someone sitting quietly beside you, refusing to leave. My first novel, Velvet Vow, was written on my phone during four hundred overnight shifts.

I live in the rain with two cats, an unreasonable number of candles, and a corkboard covered in red string. I answer every reader email myself. If you write to me, I will write back.

— Nocturne
